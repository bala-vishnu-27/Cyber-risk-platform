4RISK FACTORS CONSIDERED
  • 🎯
    CVSS Score
    Technical severity of the vulnerability (0 to 10)
  • 📈
    EPSS Score
    Probability of exploit in the future (FIRST.org)
  • 🐛
    Exploit Availability
    Whether active exploit code is publicly available
  • 🌐
    Internet Exposure
    Is the application publicly internet-facing?
  • 🏢
    Business Criticality
    How critical is this application to business?
  • 📦
    Dependency Type
    Nature of the component — Direct or Indirect
